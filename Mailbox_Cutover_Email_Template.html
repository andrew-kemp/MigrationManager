Your mailbox migration is now entering the cutover phase. This is the final step where your mailbox will be switched to the new system.

What to Expect

  • You will receive a notification in Outlook informing you that an administrator has made a change and that you should restart Outlook.
  • If prompted to sign in again after restarting, log in using your Cygnet email address and password (not NHS credentials).
  • After cutover, your email, calendar, and contacts will be available in the new mailbox environment.

Instructions for Desktop Outlook

  1. Close all open Outlook windows.
  2. Open Outlook again. If you see a notification about changes made by your administrator, click OK or Restart as prompted.
  3. When asked to sign in, use your Cygnet email address and password.
  4. Follow the Outlook setup wizard if prompted:
    • Select Microsoft 365 or Exchange account type if asked.
    • If you see multiple accounts, ensure you select your Cygnet account.
  5. Check that your email, calendar, and contacts are present and up to date.

Instructions for Company Mobile Devices

  1. If you have the Email Plus app installed, please remove/uninstall it from your device.
  2. If you do not already have the Microsoft Outlook app, please install it:
    • Android: Google Play Store - Outlook
    • iPhone: Apple App Store - Outlook
  3. Open the Outlook app and add your account:
    • Tap Add Account (or choose Get Started).
    • Enter your Cygnet email address and password.
    • Follow the wizard steps to complete setup.
    • Allow notifications and permissions if prompted.
