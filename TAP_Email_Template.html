  1. Open the Microsoft Authenticator app.

  3. Select Work or school account.
  4. When prompted, choose Sign in with Temporary Access Pass and enter your TAP: {TAP}
  5. Complete the instructions to finish adding your account and setting up your Passkey.

If you already have your work account and use Authenticator for MFA:

  1. Open the Microsoft Authenticator app on your mobile device.
  2. Tap your existing work or school account in the app.
  3. Tap Set up Passkey (or "Add a Passkey").
  4. When prompted, select Sign in with Temporary Access Pass and enter your TAP: {TAP}
  5. Follow the on-screen instructions to finish adding your Passkey to this account.

Tip: If you do not see the "Add a Passkey" option, make sure your Microsoft Authenticator app is updated to the latest version.
